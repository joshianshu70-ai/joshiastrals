# Joshi Astrals · proposed holder artwork license
DRAFT FOR REVIEW — not effective, not a license grant, not published terms.

## What ownership means
Owning an NFT records control of a token. It does not by itself transfer copyright, a trademark, ownership of a blockchain, or equity in The House of Joshi. This proposed license would describe the additional permission granted to current holders.

## Proposed permission
Subject to final approval, the licensor would grant the lawful current holder of an authentic Joshi Astrals NFT a worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free permission to display, reproduce, adapt and use the associated artwork for personal and commercial projects. No proposed revenue cap. Examples: profile pictures, personal wallpapers, prints and merchandise featuring the held Astral. This would cover only rights the licensor actually possesses and can grant.

## Scope and exclusions
Permission would cover the artwork associated with the held token and adaptations of that artwork. It would not transfer copyright ownership or grant use of The House of Joshi / Joshi Astrals names, logos or marks as a business identity, endorsement, or source of unrelated products. Plain factual attribution would be permitted. Holders would not receive rights to other tokens' artwork, third-party rights, or exclusive rights against other holders.

Artwork is made with generative AI assistance and human curation, composition and design. Protectability and available rights can vary by work and jurisdiction. Do not promise exclusive copyright or represent that every generated component is independently copyrightable. Human-authored code, writing, curation and brand elements require their own rights assessment.

## Transfers
The proposed permission would apply only while the holder lawfully controls the authentic token. On transfer, the prior holder would stop making new commercial products using it and the successor would receive the permission if final terms so provide. Proposed wind-down: 60 days to sell existing physical inventory produced before transfer, without implying current ownership. Existing third-party arrangements need specific review before agreeing to them.

## Representation
Do not impersonate The House of Joshi, imply an endorsement, misrepresent ownership, or use the artwork in violation of applicable law. Separate legal obligations apply to third-party marks, publicity and other protected rights.

## Utility and service availability
The local Astral Studio currently generates profile pictures, wallpapers and banners. Its ownership check requires a configured official Arc contract. Public artwork and metadata are not secret; verification is a convenience, not a promise of private content. Hosted service terms, availability, support contact and any discontinuation policy must be finalized before launch. No promise of permanent server operation, future drops, financial returns, royalties to holders or guaranteed resale liquidity.
