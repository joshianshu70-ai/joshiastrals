# Joshi Astrals · Site and mint terms

## Who we are
Joshi Astrals is presented by The House of Joshi. Contact support@thehouseofjoshi.com. Official website: joshiastrals.thehouseofjoshi.com. Official Arc contract: 0x402f4ead8909145a57a45eeb27446111e6738c48. Version 1.0, prepared 16 October 2026; effective on publication at this website.

## Sale status and price
The collection has 7,777 NFTs: a planned 200-NFT House allocation and 7,577 public mints. The current public plan is one sale at 25 USDC per NFT, with no buyer whitelist and a planned limit of two public mints per wallet. No launch date has been announced. Final availability, start time and transaction details are shown on the official OpenSea Drop. Review those details and your wallet confirmation before minting. Network gas is additional.

## Minting and artwork
Mint through the official OpenSea Drop, not through an artwork preview on this site. Standard SeaDrop assigns sequential token IDs. The creator locally shuffled the public artwork for reveal and knows that mapping; this is not verified randomness. Customizing or previewing an artwork does not reserve it. The House-only stage must complete before public sales to preserve the intended team allocation.

## Ownership and royalties
An NFT records token ownership. Artwork permissions are described separately in the holder artwork license. The initial contract resale royalty is 7% to the House wallet; payment depends on marketplace rules and is not guaranteed. Royalties are not an additional primary mint charge. The standard contract owner can administer supply, metadata, royalties and Drop settings.

## Wallet connection and owner tools
You control your wallet and approve its actions. We never need your seed phrase or private key. Astral Studio opens after reveal, checks the connected wallet’s token ownership and requires a short-lived, one-use signed message for downloads. This signature is not a payment transaction. Transfers or wallet changes remove access for the former holder. Smart-contract wallet signatures are not currently supported by the download service.

## Data and privacy
Wallet addresses, token holdings and onchain activity are public blockchain data. The site sends a wallet address to Arc RPC services to discover holdings and checks ownership for exports. Temporary signature challenges are held in server memory and expire. Wallet connection is provided by Reown AppKit; OpenSea and RPC providers have their own terms and privacy practices. This site does not ask you to create an account or provide a seed phrase. Contact support for privacy questions.
