    YOUR COLLECTION, ONCHAIN
    Give your Astral a home.
    Deploy and manage the Joshi Astrals collection. New contracts start closed with no phase active. Phase quantities, prices and the 7% royalty are fixed in the contract. Only the owner can activate phases, pause the sale or mint the House reserve.
    100 basis points = 1%. ERC-2981 signals royalty information; marketplaces decide whether to pay it. Treasury and royalty settings are fixed at deployment.
    Deploy once after finalized metadata is uploaded. No phase opens during deployment; activate Phase 1 separately. The base URI is immutable. Deployment opens a wallet transaction.
    Mint phases
    Phase prices are fixed at 25 / 35 / 45 USDC. You can activate only the next phase, after the current phase sells out. The 200-NFT House reserve is separate from public phase caps. Public mint requests receive verified random IDs after a service callback and ordered settlement. The buyer then claims the assigned NFTs. House reserve IDs are excluded from the random public pool.
    Phase 1 reserved-seat list
    Public minting stays open to everyone. Up to 50 registered wallets receive 2 protected seats each, at 25 USDC per NFT plus randomness fees and gas. A total of 100 Phase 1 seats is held initially, including not-yet-registered slots. Listed wallets can also buy general seats. The remaining 677 seats are general availability until protected seats are used or released.
    One wallet and 0–2 allowance per line. Maximum 50 distinct wallet slots. Removal does not reclaim a registration slot, and allowances cannot be reduced below already-used protected seats. Adding addresses reserves seats; it never blocks other wallets from general availability.
    Release is permanent: all unclaimed protected seats become public, and the list can no longer be edited. Release unused seats to let Phase 1 sell out before Phase 2.
    No official deployment yet. The list is empty; 100 seats will be held on deployment.
    House reserve · 200 NFTs
    Fixed IDs #7577–#7777, excluding zodiac #7655. Reserve mints go to the configured reserve wallet and do not reduce public phase allocations.
    Reserve batch minting uses the fixed reserve list; selecting a preview does not change it.
    Transfer collection ownership
    Two-step transfer: the current owner proposes a wallet, then that wallet accepts. Treasury and royalty recipient remain unchanged.
