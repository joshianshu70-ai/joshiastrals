Privacy notice

How wallet verification, website providers and support requests handle information.

Who to contact

The House of Joshi is the creator name supplied for this project. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@thehouseofjoshi.com. This notice does not assert a registered company status or jurisdiction.

Information used

Wallet addresses, token IDs and blockchain holdings are public data. We use the connected address to discover holdings and verify ownership. Your browser sends the transaction you approve to your wallet and the Arc network. Support email may include your email address, message and transaction hash. Do not send recovery phrases or private keys.

Download verification

Owner downloads use short-lived wallet-signed requests. In the Vercel implementation, a shared nonce marker expires after three minutes to prevent request reuse; signed challenges expire after two minutes. Local development uses temporary in-memory challenges. Failed or expired verification does not grant downloads. Public blockchain records are not deleted when a temporary request expires.

Providers and technical data

Vercel hosts deployed pages and functions. Reown AppKit provides wallet connection. Arc RPC services receive queries about addresses and tokens. OpenSea provides its separate marketplace and Drop. Revealed artwork downloads may use the public ipfs.io gateway. Google Fonts supplies website fonts. Shared verification storage may use Upstash. These providers may process IP addresses, device/request details and operational logs under their own policies.

Browser storage and analytics

Wallet connection providers may use cookies or browser storage to remember sessions. The project’s AppKit configuration disables its analytics feature; this does not mean third-party platforms collect no operational data. This site does not add an advertising tracker or mailing-list form through these pages. Disconnect through your wallet interface and manage browser storage in your browser settings.

Retention and requests

Temporary verification expiry is described above. Support correspondence and provider logs may be retained for handling requests, security and applicable legal obligations; no universal retention period is promised here. Ask support about access, correction or deletion where applicable. Public blockchain data and third-party records cannot necessarily be changed or erased by this project.

Sharing and transfers

Information is processed as necessary by the providers above and may be handled in countries different from yours. We do not offer wallet data for sale through this website. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to investigate abuse, subject to applicable obligations.

Provider policies

Read Vercel, Reown and OpenSea privacy notices for their own services. This page will be updated if the project adds new data collection.
