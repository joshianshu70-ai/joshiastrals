About Joshi Astrals

Rooted in Earth. Written in the stars.

Art and identity

Joshi Astrals brings together 7,777 cosmic companions presented by The House of Joshi. Four families—Earth, Water, Air and Fire—combine lavender stone, sea glass, iridescent pearl and obsidian with nature, pattern and personality. Artwork uses generative AI assistance with human curation, composition and design.

Nature, mathematics and symbolism

Spirals, branching structures and repeating patterns inform the art. Zodiac signs and celestial imagery are symbolic identities rather than scientific predictions. The Celestial Twelve includes one artwork edition per zodiac sign in the public pool.

Supply and allocation

The current plan is 200 House-allocation NFTs and 7,577 public mints. The intended House/community/collaboration uses are not vesting or transfer locks. All twelve zodiac artworks remain in the public artwork pool. Standard contract administration can change supply and sale settings.

What is built

The project includes a layered artwork explorer, trait-frequency rankings, a direct SeaDrop public mint interface and an OpenSea entry point. Owner Studio is implemented but remains gated until the official reveal, verified mapping and backend setup. These are separate from any promise of perpetual service or future benefits.

What is not promised

No floor price, investment return, dividends, resale liquidity or guaranteed future drops. Local artwork shuffling is known to the creator and is not verified randomness. See FAQ, risks and the artwork license for details.
