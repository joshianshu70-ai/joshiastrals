Frequently asked questions

Answers about minting, artwork, ownership and wallet safety.

What is Joshi Astrals?

7,777 digital art collectibles by The House of Joshi on Arc, with Earth, Water, Air and Fire families, mathematical patterns, celestial symbolism and Web3 personality.

Where can I mint?

The website Public mint panel and the official OpenSea collection use the same SeaDrop contract. Public minting is available only when the onchain stage is configured and active; an existing collection page alone does not mean a sale is open.

What is the price and wallet limit?

The approved public plan is 25 USDC per NFT plus gas, up to two public mints per wallet. The website checks live settings before enabling a transaction. Wallet usage is cumulative onchain across both entry points and prior mint stages; transferring an NFT does not reset it.

Is there a buyer whitelist?

No buyer whitelist is planned. The separate House-only free allocation is 200 NFTs and must finish before public sales. This leaves 7,577 intended public mints.

Can I choose my artwork before minting?

No. Customization and gallery previews do not select or reserve the artwork you receive. SeaDrop assigns sequential token IDs, and public artwork is shuffled locally for reveal. The creator knows the mapping; this is not verified randomness.

What is the Celestial Twelve?

Twelve artwork editions, one for each zodiac sign, are in the public pool. Zodiac imagery is artistic symbolism, not a scientific prediction or investment signal.

Why might the mint button be disabled?

The public stage may be unset, not started, ended, sold out, or require launch configuration review. Your wallet may have reached its limit. Read the live message in the mint panel; do not send funds directly to a contract address.

When do images and owner tools unlock?

Official artwork appears after the creator completes the OpenSea reveal. Astral Studio remains locked until the revealed artwork mapping is verified and its backend is configured. Holders can then verify their wallet to make PFPs, banners and wallpapers.

What rights come with my NFT?

The token does not transfer copyright or collection trademarks. Personal and commercial artwork permission for lawful current holders is described in the holder artwork license.

Does buying guarantee profit or future benefits?

No. Price, resale demand, liquidity and future services are not guaranteed. An NFT can lose all value. This project does not provide financial advice or promise dividends, investment returns or guaranteed future drops. Read the risk notice.

Are royalties guaranteed?

The initial contract signals a 7% resale royalty to the House wallet. Actual payment depends on the marketplace and transaction. The standard owner can administer royalty settings; this is not an immutable guarantee or a payout to holders.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Blockchain transactions generally cannot be reversed by this website. Contact support promptly with the transaction hash if something went wrong. Applicable consumer and refund rights remain unaffected; this is not a blanket exclusion of statutory rights.

Will support ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Use the published support email. We will not ask for a private key, recovery phrase or a payment to unlock a refund or prize.
