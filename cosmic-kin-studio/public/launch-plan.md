# Joshi Astrals launch plan

The House of Joshi presents 7,777 cosmic companions. No official contract or sale is live.

1. Review the final artwork, exact trait counts and all twelve zodiac editions. Pin 1024px WebP images and finalized metadata to durable public IPFS. Preserve original masters. The deployment metadata base URI is immutable.
2. Finalize creator/legal identity, contact, artwork rights and sale terms. Publish the official domain and verified contract addresses.
3. Rehearse on Arc Testnet: D20DAO fee quotation, paid request, real proof delivery, FIFO assignment, claim, timeout refund and callback retry. Verify all provider proxy implementations against the pinned SDK. Local mock tests do not replace this rehearsal or an independent security review.
4. Configure Phase 1's reserved-seat list. There are 50 lifetime wallet slots with up to 2 protected seats each, inside the 777 Phase 1 allocation. 100 seats are initially held; the other 677 are general availability. Everyone may mint from general availability. Listed wallets may also buy general seats. Owner can permanently release unused protected seats so Phase 1 sells out.
5. Launch through the website. Phase quantities/prices: 777 at 25 USDC, 2,000 at 35 USDC, 4,800 at 45 USDC. The randomness service fee and gas are additional. The fixed free-price wallet pays no NFT price, but still pays service fees/gas. Phase transition requires booked allocation and resolved pending assignments.
6. Requests receive unique verified-random public token IDs. Metadata/artwork follows each assigned ID. House reserve IDs remain excluded; all twelve zodiac IDs remain public. A preview cannot reserve an outcome. Only the buyer can claim the assigned NFTs. Settlement and claim require separate transactions.
7. Mint payments remain in escrow until assignment. Expired unfulfilled requests can recover the NFT price as buyer refund credit; service-fee refunds follow the provider's rules. Accepted words cannot be rerolled. Failed receivers can claim to a different receiver without changing IDs.
8. Owner studio fetches current claimed NFTs from the connected wallet, including transfers. Only owners can create/download their NFT's wallpapers, PFPs and banners through the signed ownership-check flow. Public artwork remains viewable outside that feature.
9. Confirm OpenSea indexing after deployment/claims. Royalties signal 7%; no enforced royalties or SeaDrop direct OpenSea minting is promised.

House reserve: 200 fixed non-zodiac editions, #7577–#7777 excluding #7655, minted only by owner to the immutable payout wallet. Intended uses: 100 House, 50 community, 50 collaborations; these are not vesting locks. Public pool: 7,577 random IDs. Owner controls phases/sale, list updates before release, reserve minting and withdrawal of earned proceeds. Two-step ownership transfer; renunciation disabled. Treasury and metadata URI stay immutable.

No launch date, investment return, price floor or independent audit is announced. Pinning, terms, live Testnet rehearsal and deployment remain pending.
