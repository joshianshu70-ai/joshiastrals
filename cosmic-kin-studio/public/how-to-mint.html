How to mint

Two entry points. One official collection on Arc.

1. Check the live stage

Open the Public mint tab on the home page or the official OpenSea collection. A live mint requires an active onchain public stage. The site stays disabled if launch configuration is incomplete.

2. Connect your wallet

Use the wallet connection dialog and select Arc mainnet. Have enough native USDC for the approved 25 USDC per NFT and additional network gas. The public plan is up to two per wallet, counted onchain across both entry points and previous stages.

3. Review and approve

Choose one or two Astrals if available. The website checks the price, supply, fee recipient and wallet usage before requesting the transaction. Read the risk notice, terms and artwork license. Review the final request in your wallet; never send funds directly to an address.

4. Wait for confirmation

Use the Arc transaction link to track the mint. After confirmation, the NFTs belong to the wallet that minted them. Artwork previews do not determine which token artwork you receive. OpenSea indexing may take time.

5. After reveal

Once the official reveal and verified mapping are ready, holders can connect to Astral Studio to create personal artwork exports. Ownership is rechecked for downloads. If a transaction is pending or unclear, inspect it on the explorer before trying again.
