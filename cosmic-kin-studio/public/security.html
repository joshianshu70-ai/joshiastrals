Wallet safety

Check the destination. Read the request. Keep your recovery phrase private.

Verify the collection

Use joshiastrals.thehouseofjoshi.com and the linked official OpenSea collection. Confirm the Arc contract address 0x402f4ead8909145a57a45eeb27446111e6738c48. A familiar name, logo or social message does not prove authenticity.

Read mint transactions

Website public minting calls the canonical SeaDrop address 0x00005EA00Ac477B1030CE78506496e8C2dE24bf5 for the official collection. Check Arc mainnet, quantity, value and gas before signing. This mint uses native USDC on Arc; it does not require an NFT operator approval or an ERC-20 spending approval from this website. Do not send funds directly to either contract.

Understand download signatures

Astral Studio requests a short-lived message naming the website, network, contract, token ID and expiry. Its owner-download message is not a payment transaction. Read it before signing. Never assume all signatures are harmless: marketplace orders and approvals can have financial consequences.

Protect your wallet

Never share recovery phrases or private keys. Treat unsolicited support messages, fake airdrops and links promising guaranteed rewards as suspicious. Use the support email published on this website to verify a concern. We will not ask you to install remote-access software or pay to unlock a refund.

If something goes wrong

Stop interacting with the suspicious site and contact your wallet provider through its official channel. Report the incident to project support with public transaction details. We cannot promise recovery, reverse blockchain transfers or erase public chain records.
